# Variants R2 — Dear Human: The AI Breakup Line (resubmit blind round)

Judge criteria (lexicographic, RULES.txt): (1) real breakup message from an
AI agent to its human; (2) lands as an ending (loss/relief/finality);
(3) sounds like the machine, habits used with restraint; (4) turns once,
sharply, twist in the last few words; (5) one clean line, every word earns
its place. Margin scoring: a decisive win (margin 5) is worth 1.0/call,
a hair win (margin 1) only 0.6/call — pick the variant that wins TELAK.
